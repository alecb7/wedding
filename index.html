The Venue

Sheen Falls Lodge sits on the banks of the Sheen River just outside Kenmare, where the falls meet Kenmare Bay. Following the church ceremony, the drinks reception, dinner and dancing all take place at the lodge.

Travel & Stay

Kerry Airport is slightly closer to Kenmare, though Cork Airport has more flights and generally better car hire availability. We would recommend hiring a car, as the drive through the Kerry countryside is part of the occasion.

Where to Stay

To book a room, please call Sheen Falls Lodge directly and mention that you are attending Zara and Alec's wedding. Our wedding rate is €570 for two nights or €290 for one night.

If you would like recommendations for anywhere else in Kerry or Cork, please get in touch and we will happily point you in the right direction.
